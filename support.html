Support for WeatherLog

WeatherLog · Last updated: 2026-11-02

What WeatherLog is

WeatherLog is a personal, single-user command-line job published by RodRCode LLC. It records public weather readings for Oak Hill Splash Park from the City of Carrollton’s public Perry Weather widget and backs them up to a private folder in its owner’s Google Drive, using only the Google Drive drive.file scope (files WeatherLog creates). It has no app, website login, or other users.

Revoke access

Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, select WeatherLog, and remove its access.

Delete data

  • Delete WeatherLog’s folder in Google Drive.
  • Stop the scheduled job and delete the local weather log files.

Details are in the Privacy Policy.
