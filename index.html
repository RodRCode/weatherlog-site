WeatherLog

The application name is WeatherLog.

Official home page for WeatherLog, a personal weather-logging backup tool published by [PUBLISHER].

Purpose of WeatherLog

WeatherLog is a small, single-user command-line job. It regularly records public weather readings for Oak Hill Splash Park in Carrollton, Texas, from the City of Carrollton’s public Perry Weather widget, and backs those readings up to a private folder in its owner’s own Google Drive.

WeatherLog has no user interface, no sign-up, and no other users. It is not a mobile app or a website. The only person who signs in is its owner.

Google user data

WeatherLog uses the Google Drive API with a single scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which lets it see and manage only the files and folders WeatherLog itself creates. It cannot read any other Drive files, and it does not access Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, or profile information. See the Privacy Policy for details.

WeatherLog is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with Google, the City of Carrollton, or Perry Weather.
